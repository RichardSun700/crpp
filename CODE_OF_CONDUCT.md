# Code of Conduct

CRPP discussions include people with unequal workplace power and different legal, technical, and lived experience. Participants must be respectful, evidence-oriented, and careful not to pressure anyone to disclose private employment or personal information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, retaliation, doxxing, exposure of confidential context, and bad-faith misrepresentation are not tolerated.

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or refer a matter to an independent conduct reviewer. Reports should use the private contact listed in the GitHub Security or organization profile once established. Until then, do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

This concise draft will be replaced through a public CEP with a complete enforcement and appeal process before the first release candidate.
