# CRPP Project Charter

CRPP develops an open, vendor-neutral protocol for context rights, portability, provenance, joint governance, and controlled post-project access.

The project will:

- publish immutable, versioned specifications and conformance suites;
- represent worker, independent contributor, organization, privacy/security/legal, implementation, and public-interest perspectives;
- keep normative decisions, votes, conflicts of interest, and meeting records public;
- prevent any sponsor, founder, employer, or vendor from purchasing protocol control;
- separate technical conformance from legal certification.

Memova is an initiating reference participant. It has no permanent seat or veto.
